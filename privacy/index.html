Privacy Policy

Prep Mate - Tech Interview — Last updated: 3 February 2026

This policy explains how Prep Mate - Tech Interview (“we”, “the app”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our Android app distributed on Google Play. It is intended to meet transparency requirements described in Google Play’s app content and privacy and user-data expectations.

1. Data controller

The developer identified on the app’s Google Play listing is responsible for this processing. For privacy requests, use the contact details shown on that listing.

2. Data we collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may process:

  • Account data — e.g. name, email, or user identifiers you provide when you sign in (including via third-party sign-in providers).
  • App usage data — e.g. learning progress, selected technologies or difficulty preferences, and notes you save in the in-app journal.
  • Content you submit — text you send to AI/chat features so we can return a response.
  • Technical & security data — limited diagnostics, device/app identifiers as needed for operation, fraud prevention, and support.
  • Advertising-related data — if the app shows ads, ad partners may collect or receive data under their own policies and your device settings.

3. How we use data

  • Provide and improve app features (quizzes, learning, journal, AI assistance).
  • Authenticate users, secure the service, and enforce limits and policies.
  • Comply with law and Google Play requirements.
  • Where applicable, serve or measure ads in line with platform rules and consent where required.

4. Who may access data

Access is limited to us, our service providers (e.g. hosting, authentication, analytics or ad SDKs as used in the app), and parties if required by law. Each third party processes data under its own policy where it acts as a separate controller or processor. We do not sell your personal information as a commodity; standard ad/analytics SDKs may use data as described in their policies.

5. Sharing & transfers

We share data with providers strictly as needed to run the app. Some providers may be in countries other than yours; where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards (e.g. standard contractual clauses) as offered by those services.

6. Retention

We keep data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. You may request deletion of your account or certain data where the app or Play listing provides a contact or in-app option.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. Children

If the app’s target audience on Google Play includes children under applicable age thresholds (e.g. under 13 where U.S. COPPA applies), this policy applies to them too, and you must comply with Play’s Families and data policies. Do not use the app outside the age group you declare in Play Console.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. EEA/UK GDPR), you may have rights to access, rectify, delete, restrict, object, or port your data. Contact us via the Play store listing to exercise these rights.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above will change. Continued use of the app after updates may mean you accept the revised policy where allowed by law.

For Play Console, host this page at a stable https URL and enter that URL under Policy and programs → App content → Privacy policy, as described in Google Play Help.
