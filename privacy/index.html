Privacy Policy

Prep Mate - Tech Interview, published on Google Play as “IT Interview Questions” (“App”, “Service”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) — Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains how Prep Mate - Tech Interview — listed on Google Play under the app name “IT Interview Questions” — respects your privacy and processes personal data when you use our Android app distributed on Google Play. It reflects Google Play transparency expectations and privacy and user-data guidance.

1. Data controller

The developer identified on the app’s Google Play listing is responsible for this processing. For privacy, deletion, and rights requests you may use:

  • the developer contact email on that listing; and
  • support@prepmatetech.com

2. Data we collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may process:

Account information

  • email address, display name where applicable;
  • username or profile identifiers;
  • authentication identifiers used to sign you in (including third-party providers such as Google).

Usage information

  • quiz progress, completed lessons or modules, scored attempts;
  • streaks, achievements, and gamification state;
  • selected technologies or difficulty preferences;
  • notes you save in the in-app journal;
  • general app interactions needed to run features;
  • crash reports and diagnostics to improve stability.

Content you submit

  • text you send to AI or chat features so we can return a response;
  • quiz/interview questions, answer options, explanations, and code snippets you submit through Creator or contribution features for review — see “Published Submitted Content” under §7 for what happens to this content if it is approved and published, including after account deletion.

Device & technical information

  • device model, operating system version, app version;
  • limited diagnostics and anonymous or pseudonymous identifiers as needed for operation, security, and support.

Subscription information

Premium subscriptions are processed by Google Play. Google handles payment instruments; we do not store full payment card details on our servers. We may receive subscription status tied to your account from the store.

Advertising-related data

If the app shows ads, partners may collect or receive data under their own policies and your device or account settings.

3. How we use information

  • provide and improve the Service (quizzes, learning, journal, AI assistance);
  • personalize learning experiences where the product supports it;
  • maintain accurate user progress and synchronize it across sessions;
  • authenticate users, enforce limits (including subscription entitlements where applicable), and secure the Service;
  • process subscriptions and premium access in coordination with Google Play;
  • detect abuse or fraud;
  • improve app stability and performance using diagnostics;
  • comply with law and Google Play requirements;
  • where applicable, serve or measure ads in line with platform rules and consent where required.

4. AI-powered features

Some features may use AI providers (for example OpenAI) to produce educational explanations or assistance.

  • When you submit text to AI features, content may be processed by those providers according to their terms and privacy notices.
  • Do not submit passwords, payment data, national ID numbers, health data, or other sensitive personal information through AI chat or explanation flows.

5. Who may access data & analytics

Access is limited to us and service providers strictly as needed—such as authentication, hosting, analytics SDKs as configured in the app, Firebase (where used), AI vendors, billing via Google Play, and law enforcement where required.

Typical categories include Google Play Services and related Google platform services, Firebase, analytics vendors, infrastructure / cloud hosts, and AI services.

Each vendor may process limited technical information under its own policy. We cannot control outages or policy changes by third parties, but choose providers appropriate to delivering the Service.

We do not sell your personal information as a commodity; advertising or analytics SDKs process data only as disclosed in those products’ notices.

6. Sharing, transfers & international users

We share personal data only as described above. Some processors may operate in countries other than yours.

Your information may be processed or stored internationally where our providers locate systems; where GDPR or similar law applies, we rely on appropriate safeguards offered by vendors (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) when required.

7. Retention & deletion

We retain data only as long as necessary to operate the Service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

To request deletion of your account or associated personal data we hold as controller, email support@prepmatetech.com or use contact options on Google Play—and see our Account & data deletion page for what we delete by default. We aim to respond within about 30 days; complex or legally restricted cases may take longer.

Published Submitted Content. If you contribute quiz questions or similar content through Creator features and it is reviewed and approved for publication, it becomes part of the public question bank used by other learners, under the license described in our Terms of Service. Deleting your account removes your personal data and disconnects your identity from that content, but the previously-published content itself may remain part of the Service — it is not automatically deleted along with your account, unless removal is required by law or the content itself constitutes personal data about you or a third party. Submissions that were never approved, or were rejected during review, are deleted and are not affected by this.

8. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect your information. However, no transmission or storage is 100 % secure or error-free.

9. Children’s privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13 (or younger than the minimum age required in your jurisdiction for lawful consent).

The app follows the age suitability shown on Google Play and, where COPPA/Families policies apply, we observe Google Play Families requirements alongside applicable national law.

Parents and guardians should supervise sign-in, purchases, AI use, and any child access.

10. Your rights

Depending on your location (including EEA / UK GDPR, U.S. state laws where applicable), you may have rights to:

  • access copies of certain data;
  • correct inaccuracies;
  • request deletion (“right to be forgotten”) of your personal data, subject to legal exceptions and to §7’s treatment of already-published Submitted Content;
  • restrict processing or object to certain processing;
  • data portability;
  • object to processing where local law permits.

Send requests via developer contact on Google Play or to support@prepmatetech.com; see §7 on deletion timelines.

11. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically. The “Last updated” date reflects the latest substantive revision. Continued use of the Service after changes become effective constitutes acceptance where permitted by law.
